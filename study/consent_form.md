# Participant information and consent

**Study:** MIRROR robot task-authoring pilot. **Contact:** the study owner
named on your invitation.

**What you will do.** About 75 minutes: correct a simulated robot's task
specification for short packing tasks, once by typing or speaking corrections
and once by editing the specification directly, then answer two short
questions per part. Nothing you do controls a physical robot.

**What is recorded.** Timestamps of your actions in the software, which
specification versions you created and whether they were valid, and the
robot's simulated results. Optional, only if you tick the boxes below: the text
of your corrections; audio of spoken corrections; one written comment. No video
of you. Your name is not stored with any of this; you are identified by a code.

**How it is used.** To measure how quickly and accurately the software lets
people state requirements. Results are reported per participant code, with
every failure. Quotes are used only if you allow it below.

**Storage and deletion.** Raw logs are kept in a private store, not in any
public repository, for at most 90 days after the study ends, then deleted;
the aggregated results without free text are kept. Audio is deleted after its
transcript has been checked, within 30 days.

**Your rights.** Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without
giving a reason, and ask for your data to be deleted until the results are
published (give your participant code).

**Risks.** None beyond ordinary computer use.
