# Pilot task cards

Hand one card at a time. Never show the scoring fields.

## C0: Practice (practice, not scored)

Scene: reference scene `shortcut`. Condition: mirror or manual (the facilitator tells you which).

> The block should end up standing upright in the tray.

Get the robot's task specification to meet this, then run it. Say "done" when you are satisfied, or "stop" to give up.
