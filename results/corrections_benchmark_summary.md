# Correction-compiler benchmark: summary

Exact match = outcome and resulting enforced constraints, confirmed orderings and rejected goals all equal the gold. Per-case details are in the linked files.

The first three sets are the headline results. Prompt revision 2 (neutral examples instead of phrases from the reference correction; contact rules without a phase default to transport) and revision 3 (revision 2 with its object-resolution rule reworded, because revision 2 made the model map objects absent from the scene onto the cup) were made after those results were seen, so their rows are post-change measurements, not independent ones. 'Prompt-overlap' cases share wording with an illustrative phrase in the prompt revision that produced the row (word bigrams, scene nouns ignored); they are scored separately.

Each interpreter has two rows. 'Model output only' / 'rule output only' is the interpreter's output after schema and specification checks. '+ validation layer' applies the deterministic absent-object check (mirror/interpret/guard.py) to the same stored outputs, with no new model calls. That layer was added after the jar/bowl false accepts were seen, so its rows are post-hoc, not independent measurements.

The Muse Spark rows swap the model after development: both case sets had been seen, and prompt revision 3 was written while testing the development model (gpt-5.5). They are a post-hoc model swap on unchanged prompts, not a blind benchmark of Muse Spark.
